Our Story

Building Trust Since 2014

BuildMate was founded with a simple mission — to provide quality construction materials at fair prices, with exceptional service. We started as a small local supplier and have grown to serve thousands of customers across the region.

From residential homes to large commercial projects, our cement blocks and bricks have been the foundation of countless buildings. We take pride in the quality of our products and the relationships we've built with our customers.
